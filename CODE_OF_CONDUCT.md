# Code of conduct

Baithak is named after the room where people sit together and talk. Its issues, pull requests
and discussions should feel like that room: people with different backgrounds, languages and
levels of experience, treated as welcome guests.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas and code, not about people. Explain why, and accept being wrong.
- Give feedback that helps: specific, about the change, and with a way forward.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Respect people's time and privacy.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Insults, slurs, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private, including continuing to contact someone who asked you to
  stop.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their
  permission.
- Posting real messages, files or personal data from a workspace in an issue, test or seed data.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews and any channel where you represent the project. It also applies to public behaviour
elsewhere that is directed at people in this project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, email
[support@ahmershah.dev](mailto:support@ahmershah.dev) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports
are read by the maintainer, Syed Ahmer Shah, and kept private. You'll get an answer within
7 days. If the report is about the maintainer, say so in the subject and it will be handled
without them having a say in the outcome.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what to do after hearing from the people involved, and may:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and possibly a public apology.
2. **Warn:** a warning with consequences for continuing, and a period of no contact with the
   people involved.
3. **Suspend:** a temporary ban from all project spaces.
4. **Ban:** a permanent ban from all project spaces.
